# gold

In the game: "some gold", worth 11 points on the grassy bank.

Bars rather than coins, so the gold isn't mistaken for the penny. Three bars, so it looks different from the silver's single bar.

## Prompt

> Some gold: a small stack of three shining gold bars, old and slightly worn. A treasure.
