# lost-tunnels

Shown for: The underground clue rooms: "You are lost…" / "You are too weak but you get a clue…" (clue-mask, clue-plank, clue-spell).

## Prompt

> Lost in a maze of dark underground tunnels that all look the same, branching away into blackness. In the middle, on a rock, lies a small curled **scrap of parchment**, softly lit by a single shaft of faint light: a clue. Lonely and dreamlike.
