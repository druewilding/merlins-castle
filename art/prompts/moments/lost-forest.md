# lost-forest

Shown for: The forest clue rooms: "You are lost but you find a clue: …" (clue-silver, clue-ring, clue-rope).

## Prompt

> Lost deep in a misty forest at dusk. Tall dark trees fade into thick silver mist on every side, and there's no path anywhere. In the middle, on an old tree stump, lies a small curled **scrap of parchment**, softly lit, as if left there for you: a clue. Lonely and dreamlike, not frightening.
