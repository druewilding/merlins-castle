# death-stumble

Shown for: "You fall over in the dark." (dark-tunnels, going south without the lamp)

There's no hole here: you just trip in the pitch dark. So the picture is almost all black, which makes it different from the other deaths.

## Prompt

> Deep underground in the same tunnels, in almost total darkness: the picture is nearly all blue-black. Only the faintest outlines of rough, damp rock can be made out, catching a last glint of light. On the uneven tunnel floor, a few pebbles skitter away and a single old boot lies on its side, as if someone has just tripped and fallen. A faint violet swirl of magic hangs in the dark. Quiet, eerie, a little sad.
