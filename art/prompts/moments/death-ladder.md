# death-ladder

Shown for: "The ladder slips and you fall." (mossy-steps, the balcony)

## Prompt

> Looking up at the high balcony of the old tower against the sky. The wooden ladder is toppling: its foot is still planted in the grass at the base of the tower, but its top has slipped sideways off the balcony's edge and is falling away from the wall, tilted steeply, with a clear gap of sky between its top and the balcony. The ladder is plain wood and rope, with no ivy on it. A few leaves scraped from the balcony's ivy flutter down. It is clearly falling, not floating or leaning. A moment frozen in time.
