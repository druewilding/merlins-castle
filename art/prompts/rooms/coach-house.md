# coach-house

Original text: "You are in an ancient coach house. Merlin's cloak, with a missing silver button, hangs on the wall. Something rustles."

Exits:

- west → courtyard

## Prompt

> An ancient coach house in the castle grounds. Dusty golden light falls through gaps in the timber walls onto straw and cobwebs. An old, ornate red coach with big spoked wheels stands in the middle, its paint a little faded (the same coach seen from the courtyard). On the back wall, clearly visible, hangs **Merlin's deep blue cloak with faint silver stars, one silver button visibly missing** from its row of buttons. Something rustles in the straw in a dark corner: a hint of movement, nothing seen.
>
> - West (left): the whole left-hand wall is a wide doorway, its two heavy wooden doors swung open. Through it, as if looking out sideways, we see the sunny courtyard with its well and pink roses. Warm sunlight streams in from the left across the straw floor.
> - The back wall (north) and right-hand wall (east) are solid timber, with no openings.
>
> Keep the bottom quarter as plain straw-strewn cobbles.
