# merlins-lair

Original text: "This is Merlin the wizard's lair. The walls are purple and green.
A cauldron boils. The wizard comes towards you!"

Exits: north → grand hall (needs the spell; otherwise "You are turned into a frog.").

## Prompt

> The secret lair of **Merlin the wizard**, inside a castle. The stone walls
> are painted in deep purple and rich green, with shelves of old books, jars
> and bottles, and dried herbs hanging from the beams. In the upper middle a
> black iron **cauldron** bubbles over a fire, sending up glowing green steam.
>
> Merlin himself, a tall old wizard with a long silver beard and a deep blue
> robe and pointed hat covered with faint silver stars, strides **towards the
> viewer** from beside the cauldron, one hand raised as if about to cast a
> spell. His expression is stern and powerful but not monstrous.
>
> - North (top): a heavy arched doorway in the back wall, leading out to the
>   castle's grand hall.
>
> Candlelight and the cauldron's green glow, with violet-and-gold magic sparkling
> in the air. Keep the bottom quarter as plain worn flagstones.
