# moat

Original text: "You are on the North bank of a moat. Some guards are at the toll gate. A path leads North through an archway."

Exits:

- north → river-south-bank
- south → courtyard (needs the penny; blocked: "The drawbridge is up.")

The moat, toll gate and raised drawbridge are all to the south, behind the viewer. So the guards and toll gate stand in the lower part, seen from behind, and the drawbridge isn't shown.

## Prompt

> The north bank of a castle **moat**. In the lower middle, at the water's edge, stands a small striped **toll gate** with a little hut, and two castle **guards** in tabards with halberds, seen from behind, their heads turned as if they've noticed someone. Beyond them (out of the bottom of the picture) is the castle; only the moat's still water shows along the bottom edge.
>
> - North (top): a stone **archway** with a path leading through it towards a river and fields.
>
> Summer light, water lilies, reeds.
