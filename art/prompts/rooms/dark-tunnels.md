# dark-tunnels

Original text: "You are at a crossroads in the tunnels. It is very dark."

Exits:

- north → dragon-cave
- east → clue-spell
- south → beautiful-chamber (needs the lamp; blocked: "You fall over in the dark.", fatal)

It's meant to be hard to see. The lamp makes it lighter, but there's only one picture per room.

## Prompt

> A crossroads of rough tunnels deep underground. It is **very dark**: the picture is mostly deep blue-black shadow, with just enough faint light to make out the shapes of the tunnels.
>
> - North (top): a tunnel with a faint orange, flickering glow far along it (the dragon's fire).
> - West (left) and East (right): two pitch-dark tunnel mouths.
>
> Damp rock, a few glints of water. Keep the bottom quarter as plain dark tunnel floor.
