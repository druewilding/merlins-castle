## Prompt

> An underground cavern with passages to the east (right) and north (top). In front of the north passage sit a group of big, shaggy, mossy **trolls**, not fierce but expectant, holding out their hands and looking hopeful, as if asking for a tune. One holds a tiny drum.
>
> Warm torchlight. Keep the bottom quarter as plain cave floor.
