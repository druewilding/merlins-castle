# maze

Original text: "You are inside the maze at a crossroads. You can sense danger."

Exits:

- north → treasure-room (needs the rope; blocked: "You slip down a deep, deep hole.", fatal)
- east → damp-tunnels
- south → mountain
- west → trolls

The hole to the north is a trap you're meant to discover, so it isn't shown. The golden glimmer tempts players that way (the treasure room really is beyond), and the beam is a quiet clue for the rope.

## Prompt

> Inside a **maze** of high, ancient grey stone walls, open to the sky, at a crossroads. Passages lead away to the north (top), west (left) and east (right). Strange shadows; **you can sense danger**.
>
> The north passage is the most inviting way to go: its paving runs on into shadow and turns a corner, and from around that corner comes a warm golden glimmer, as if something precious lies beyond. No hole or drop is visible. An old wooden beam spans the top of the north passage, from wall to wall.
>
> Cool light, a feeling of being watched. Keep the bottom quarter as plain stone floor.
