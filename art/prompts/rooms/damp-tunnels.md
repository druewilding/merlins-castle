# damp-tunnels

Original text: "Tunnels lead West and North. The air is damp and stale."

Exits:

- north → giant
- west → maze

The same tunnels as the giant's room next door, so the same rock and warm torchlight. The damp is a detail, not the mood.

## Prompt

> A bend in the same rocky underground tunnels as the attached neighbouring tunnels, with the same rock, stalactites and warm flickering torchlight from torches in iron brackets. The air is **damp and stale**: the rock has a wet sheen, water drips from a few stalactites, and there are small puddles on the floor catching the torchlight, with a little moss in the cracks.
>
> - North (top): a tunnel opening in the middle of the back wall leads away into darkness.
> - West (left): a tunnel runs sideways and leaves the left-hand edge of the picture about halfway up.
> - East (right): solid rock wall, no opening.
>
> Warm torchlight like the neighbouring tunnels, slightly dimmer and mistier here. Keep the bottom quarter as plain tunnel floor.
