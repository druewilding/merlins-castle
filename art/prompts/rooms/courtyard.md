# courtyard

Original text: "You are in a sunny courtyard with rambling pink roses. To the North is a drawbridge. There is a coach house to the East. Southwards is a hall."

Exits:

- north → moat
- east → coach-house
- south → grand-hall

The hall to the south is behind the viewer.

## Prompt

> A sunny castle courtyard, warm and peaceful. Old stone walls are covered with **rambling pink roses**, with a stone well and cobbles warm in the sun.
>
> - North (top): a gateway in the castle wall with a great wooden **drawbridge**, lowered on its chains. Through the gateway, as in the neighbouring moat picture: the drawbridge reaches all the way across the still water of the moat, its far end resting on the opposite bank, with water lilies, and on the far bank stand a little red-and-white striped toll booth and an old stone archway, with fields beyond.
> - East (right): a big, deep old **coach house**, like a large barn, with its wooden doors standing wide open onto a spacious, dim interior with a straw-covered floor and room to walk in. A red coach stands further back inside.
>
> Bright summer afternoon light from the upper left. Keep the bottom quarter as plain sunlit cobbles.
