# beautiful-chamber

Original text: "You are in a beautiful chamber. The floor is covered in black and white tiles and the walls are gold. A lonely flute is playing."

Exits:

- north → dark-tunnels

The flute is part of the room, not an object you can take, so it floats high up, out of reach, well away from the floor where objects lie. The chamber has only one way out (north), so it is enclosed, with no corridors or arches leading off.

## Prompt

> A **beautiful chamber** deep underground: a single enclosed, round room with a low dome. The floor is covered in **black and white chequered tiles**, polished and reflective. The **walls are gold**: smooth, gleaming gold leaf and gilded panels, softly glowing in warm light. There are no corridors, arches or openings in the side walls.
>
> High up in the back wall, in a small arched alcove well above head height, a single silver **flute** floats on its own, playing by itself. A faint ribbon of glowing, shimmering music drifts down from it into the room. The flute is small and far away. Nothing lies on the floor. Lonely, magical and still.
>
> - North (top): below the alcove, a dark tunnel mouth in the back wall leads back out.
>
> Keep the bottom quarter as plain chequered floor.
