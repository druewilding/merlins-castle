# tower

Original text: "You enter a tower. It is dark and musty inside. The way out is covered by an enormous stone. A shiver runs down your spine."

Exits:

- south → mossy-steps (needs the ring; blocked: "You are enclosed.")

The sealed way out is to the south, behind the viewer, so it's suggested by light from the bottom edge.

## Prompt

> Inside an old **tower**, dark and musty: a round room of ancient stone with cobwebs, a spiral stair going up into darkness, dust in the air. The only way out, behind the viewer, has been sealed by an enormous stone: there is **no door in the picture**, just a few thin cracks of daylight glowing along the bottom edge. A shiver runs down your spine.
>
> Very dark, cold and eerie, lit only by those thin cracks of light.
