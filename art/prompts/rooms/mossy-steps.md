# mossy-steps

Original text: "To the North some mossy steps lead upwards. An overgrown path runs South. In the East you can see a high balcony."

Exits:

- north → tower (needs the apple; blocked: "A coiled snake hisses at you.")
- east → clue-spell (needs the ladder; blocked: "The balcony is too high to reach.") (a trap: "The ladder slips and you fall.")
- south → yellow-flowers

## Prompt

> To the north (top), **mossy stone steps** lead upwards to the dark doorway of an old tower. A **coiled snake** lies on the steps, hissing, blocking the way. On the right (east), high up on the tower wall, a **balcony**, far too high to reach. An overgrown path leads out of the bottom of the picture.
>
> Green, damp, overgrown, with a sense of danger. Keep the bottom quarter as plain overgrown grass.
