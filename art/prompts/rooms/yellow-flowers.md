# yellow-flowers

Original text: "The ground is covered with tiny yellow flowers. South is a rusty gate. An overgrown path leads North."

Exits:

- north → mossy-steps
- south → rusty-gate (needs the key; blocked: "It won't open.")

The rusty gate is to the south, behind the viewer. Since v2 the very top of
it peeks up from the bottom edge, so that `effects/yellow-flowers--key` can
show it opening. It's an edit of the v1 picture (kept as
`art/anchors/yellow-flowers-v1.png`), so the meadow stays as it was.

The text panel covers the picture from about 72% of the way down on wide
screens, so the gate's tips must rise to about 65%, and no higher: only the
tips should show, or it looks as if you're on the other side of the gate.

## Prompt

> Add only the **very top of the old rusty iron gate** from the last image, right in the foreground at the bottom edge of the picture, seen from behind, as if it's just behind us and we have only just stepped through it. Only the tips of its rusty spear-point spikes and the top curve of its scrolled arch rise up from the bottom edge, reaching about a third of the way up the picture and no higher. The rest of the gate, its lock and the wall it stands in are all below the bottom edge, out of sight. A few wisps of silver mist curl up around the spikes. Everything else in the picture, the meadow of yellow flowers, the bushes, the steps and the sky, stays exactly as it is.
