# mountain

Original text: "You are on a mountain. Some treasure lies to the East. A track leads South. A maze lies to the North."

Exits:

- north → maze
- east → clue-spell (a trap: "Oh dear! Merlin's trap is that way.")
- south → mountain-path

The treasure is Merlin's trap, painted as scenery, not a takeable object.

## Prompt

> High on a **mountain**, on a windswept rocky ledge with sweeping views. At the right-hand (east) edge of the picture, about halfway up, a big heap of glittering **treasure** (gold coins, goblets, jewels and an open chest) gleams temptingly at the end of a short path branching off to the right. It's a trap, but it looks lovely, and it's the brightest, most eye-catching thing in the picture. No path leads left (west). To the north (top), the entrance to a **maze** of high grey stone walls. A track leads down out of the bottom of the picture.
>
> Bright mountain light, clouds below. Keep the bottom quarter as plain rock.
