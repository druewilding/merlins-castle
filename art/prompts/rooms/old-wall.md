# old-wall

Original text: "An old wall is to the East. North is a path which winds into the mist. A small, dark tunnel goes West. To the South are some trees."

Exits:

- north → rusty-gate
- east → old-stone-wall (needs the ladder; blocked: "The wall is much too tall.")
- south → forest-crossroads
- west → cave-entrance (needs the lamp; blocked: "You can't see.")

The trees to the south are behind the viewer.

## Prompt

> The other side of the old stone wall. On the right (east) the tall, mossy **old wall**. To the north (top), a path winds away into **swirling mist**. On the left (west), a **small, dark tunnel** opens into a rocky bank.
>
> Softer, mistier light than the meadows. Keep the bottom quarter as plain grass.
