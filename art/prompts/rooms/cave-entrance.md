## Prompt

> A rocky hillside at the **entrance to a cave**. On the right (east), the cave mouth is choked with a huge **pile of dry autumn leaves**. A foot-path winds away to the left (west) through grass and bushes. In the lower middle, a dark hole in the ground leads down into an underground **tunnel**.
>
> Late afternoon summer light. Keep the bottom quarter as plain rocky grass.
