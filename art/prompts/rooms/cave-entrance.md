# cave-entrance

Original text: "You are at the entrance to a cave going East. A foot-path leads West. Southwards a tunnel goes underground."

Exits:

- east → dragon-cave (needs the broom; blocked: "A pile of leaves fills the entrance.")
- south → old-wall (needs the lamp; blocked: "It's too dark down there.")
- west → evergreen-glade

The cave is to the east, so its mouth is in the right-hand side of the picture, opening towards the middle. The tunnel goes south, towards the viewer, so it's shown as steps sinking into the ground, seen from behind.

## Prompt

> A rocky hillside at the **entrance to a cave**. A steep, mossy rock face rises along the whole right-hand (east) side of the picture. Halfway up the picture, the **cave** mouth opens in that rock face, facing left, towards the middle of the picture, and it is choked with a huge **pile of dry autumn leaves**. A foot-path runs sideways across the middle of the picture and leaves the left-hand (west) edge about halfway up.
>
> In the lower middle, just above the plain foreground, worn stone steps lead down into a dark underground **tunnel**. The steps go down towards the viewer (south), so we see them from above and behind: the top step is furthest away, and the steps sink out of sight into darkness towards the bottom of the picture, under a low stone arch whose back we see.
>
> No walls or buildings. Late afternoon summer light. Keep the bottom quarter as plain rocky grass.
