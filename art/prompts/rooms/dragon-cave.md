# dragon-cave

Original text: "You stand in a large cave. A splendid dragon puffing fire stands
in the entrance of the tunnel South. More tunnels lead West and North."

Exits: south → dark tunnels (needs water), west → cave entrance, north → giant.

The south tunnel is behind the camera, so the dragon guarding it stands in
the lower part of the picture with **its back to us**, facing into the cave.
Its head turns in profile so we can see its face and fire. The text panel
can cover its tail and feet, but not its head or wings.
