# dragon-cave

Original text: "You stand in a large cave. A splendid dragon puffing fire stands
in the entrance of the tunnel South. More tunnels lead West and North."

Exits: south → dark tunnels (needs water), west → cave entrance, north → giant.

The dragon guards the **south** tunnel, which is at the bottom of our picture.
So it stands in the lower middle of the frame, turned to face us, and rises
tall into the centre. The text panel can cover its feet, not its head.
