# river-south-bank

Original text: "You are on the South bank of a river. A forest lies to the West. To the South, through a stone archway, a golden spire is shining in the sun."

Exits:

- north → deep-river (needs the plank; blocked: "You fall in but get out safely.")
- south → moat
- west → forest-edge

The golden spire and the stone archway are to the south, behind the viewer, so the text describes them.

## Prompt

> The south bank of a wide, deep, swift **river** with foaming rapids. The river runs across the upper middle of the picture from left to right, and the far (north) bank is a grassy bank with paths. On this side, soft grass and reeds.
>
> - North (top): the river, with the far bank clearly visible beyond it. There is **no bridge**, no stepping stones and no way across: the river is too wide to jump and too deep to wade.
> - West (left): the edge of a deep green **forest**, with dark trees filling the left-hand side of the picture.
> - South (bottom): a path runs from the river's edge down through the grass and leaves the bottom edge of the picture, towards a stone archway we can't see.
>
> Bright summer light. Keep the bottom quarter as plain grass.
