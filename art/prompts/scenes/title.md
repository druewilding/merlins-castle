# title

The title screen. The game overlays "Merlin's Castle" and "By Anita Straker"
in its own font, so the image has no text.

This prompt is **self-contained**: don't paste the scene style block (its
camera and composition rules are for rooms).

## Attach two images

1. `art/anchors/grassy-bank.png`: the painting style.
2. `docs/bbc-title-screen.png`: the original 1983 title screen, for the
   castle's shape.

## Prompt (paste as it is)

> The first attached image is a **style reference**: match its painterly,
> semi-realistic storybook style, brushwork and level of detail, but not its
> content.
>
> The second attached image is the original 1983 BBC Micro title screen of the
> game "Merlin's Castle". Use its castle as **inspiration for the shape**: a
> tight cluster of tall, slender towers of different heights, some of pale
> cream stone and some of dark slate, with pointed conical roofs, small pennant
> flags, chequered stonework, tall pointed-arch windows, and a central dark
> gatehouse with an arched, studded door. Don't copy its flat blocky look:
> repaint it as a real, magical castle.
>
> Create a landscape 3:2 painting of **Merlin's castle at dusk**, seen from a
> little below, rising against a deep royal-blue twilight sky with the first
> stars appearing. The windows glow warmly with candlelight. Among the towers
> rises one slender **golden spire** catching the last of the sun. A faint
> violet-and-gold shimmer of magic hangs around the castle. Enchanted and
> inviting, a little mysterious, not dark or menacing.
>
> Keep the **bottom third** dark and quiet (shadowy ground, soft mist, no
> detail) because the title will be written there. No text, letters or
> people anywhere in the image.
