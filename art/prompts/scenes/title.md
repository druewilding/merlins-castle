# title

Inspired by the original BBC title screen (`docs/`): a cluster of tall
castle towers, some pale and some dark, with pointed roofs and pennant flags,
on a deep blue background. The game will overlay the words "Merlin's Castle"
and "By Anita Straker" in its own font.

## Prompt (paste after the scene style block; attach the anchor)

Ignore the north-facing camera rule for this one.

> Merlin's castle at dusk, seen from a little below, rising against a deep
> royal-blue twilight sky with the first stars. A cluster of tall, slender
> towers of different heights, some of pale cream stone and some of dark slate,
> with pointed roofs, chequered stonework, tall arched windows glowing
> warmly, and small pennant flags. Among them rises one slender **golden spire**
> catching the last of the sun. A great arched gate with a studded door sits
> in the middle. A faint violet-and-gold shimmer of magic hangs around the
> castle.
>
> Leave the **bottom third** as dark, quiet ground and mist, with no detail,
> because the title will be written there. No text in the image.
