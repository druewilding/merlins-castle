# deep-river--plank

Shown when: "The plank reaches to the other side." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> A long, sturdy wooden **plank** now reaches from the rocky edge of this bank out over the rushing river, pointing straight towards us (south). Its near end rests on the bank in the middle of the picture, clearly visible. It runs **straight down and out of the bottom edge of the picture**: its far end is out of sight, beyond the bottom edge, on the far bank behind us. We must not see the far end, and nothing holds it up in the picture: no rock, stone or bank under it in the water. The river keeps rushing past beneath it right down to the bottom edge. It is the plank in the second image. Nothing else in the picture changes.
