# dragon-cave--water

Shown when: "The dragon's fire turns to smoke." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> The dragon's plume of orange fire is now a harmless, billowing cloud of **grey smoke and white steam**, dripping with water. The dragon looks rather surprised, its eyes wide. The cave's light is a little cooler and steamier where the fire was. The dragon keeps exactly the same pose, colours and place.
