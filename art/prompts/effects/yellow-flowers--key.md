# yellow-flowers--key

Shown when: "The gate opens." (opens the way south)

The gate opens **into this meadow** (north), hinged at its outer pillars, so
here it swings towards us. It must match `rusty-gate--key`, the same open gate
from the other side, so make that one first.

## Prompt

> The rusty iron gate at the bottom of the picture now **stands open**. It opens into this meadow, so both halves have swung **towards us**, up into the flowers, each turning on its hinges at its own end (the left half hinged at the left, the right half at the right). Each half now stands open at an angle, its spiked top running from the bottom edge up and outwards into the flowers on its own side, foreshortened. The middle of the bottom edge, where the arch of the closed gate was, is now an open gap with a little silver mist drifting in through it. The second image shows the same open gate from the other side, so the two halves and the way they swing must agree with it. Nothing else in the picture changes.
