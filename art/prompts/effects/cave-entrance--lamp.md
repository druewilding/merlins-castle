# cave-entrance--lamp

Shown when: "You can see where you're going now!" (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> In the lower middle, just below the path, the three small worn stone steps lead down out of sight behind a big mossy mound. Now **warm lamplight glows up from below**, as if from a lamp down the steps: it lights the three steps from beneath with a soft golden glow, and the glow spills a little onto the leaves along the top edge of the mound. **Only the light changes.** The mound stays exactly as it is, covering the way down: don't cut through it, don't add more steps or an arch, and don't change the shape or size of anything. Nothing else in the picture changes.
