# mossy-steps--apple

Shown when: "The snake glides away silently." (opens the way north)

## Prompt

> The coiled snake on the mossy steps is gone: the steps are clear, and only the **tip of its tail** is slipping away into the long grass beside them. An apple core lies on the bottom step. Nothing else in the picture changes.
