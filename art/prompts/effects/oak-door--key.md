# oak-door--key

Shown when: "The door opens." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> The door behind the viewer has opened, and warm golden light comes in **from behind us**, with a faint shimmer of music. In the middle of the bottom edge of the picture, the warm glow has become **one patch of golden light** pouring in across the tunnel floor. Because the light comes from behind, it is **brightest at the very bottom edge**: the floor closest to us is lit first and most strongly, and the light spreads from there in a soft fan, with the faint long shadow of an open door in it, reaching about a third of the way up the picture and fading as it goes. Nothing between the bottom edge and the patch is in shadow. Everything outside that patch stays as it was. Keep the tunnel itself exactly the same.
