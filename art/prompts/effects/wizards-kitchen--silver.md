# wizards-kitchen--silver

Shown when: "Merlin goes to replace his button." (opens the way east)

## Prompt

> Merlin is **leaving** to replace his button. He is no longer by the hearth: that place is empty, and the pot bubbles on by itself. Instead he has **just gone out through the open east door** on the right, and is seen from behind, beyond the doorway, in the candlelit hall: smaller with distance, walking away from us, his deep blue starry robe and cloak trailing behind him and one hand raised to his cloak where the button is missing. The **doorway itself is clear**: he is past it, not standing in it, so the way east is open. Keep the kitchen and the door exactly the same.
