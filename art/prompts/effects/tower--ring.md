# tower--ring

Shown when: "The stone rolls aside." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> The enormous stone behind the viewer has rolled a little aside, and daylight comes in **from behind us**. In the middle of the bottom edge of the picture, the thin cracks of light have become **one small patch of warm daylight** pouring in over the nearest stones. Because the light comes from behind, it is **brightest at the very bottom edge**: the stone step and floor stones closest to us are lit first and most strongly, and the light spreads from there up across the floor in a soft, narrow fan, reaching about a third of the way up the picture and fading as it goes. Nothing between the bottom edge and the patch is in shadow. A few specks of dust glow in it. Everything outside that patch stays **exactly as dark and eerie** as before: the walls, windows, stair and shadows don't get any brighter. Keep the room itself exactly the same.
