# forest-witches--broom

Shown when: "The witches fly off to the moon." (opens the way west)

## Prompt

> The witches have **flown off to the moon**: the ring by the little fire is empty, and the witches are small dark silhouettes on broomsticks rising above the treetops on the left, towards a pale daytime moon. The rabbits have relaxed, one nibbling grass, one washing its ears. Nothing else changes.
