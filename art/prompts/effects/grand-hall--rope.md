# grand-hall--rope

Shown when: "The goblins look worried!" (opens the way west)

## Prompt

> The goblin who was peeking round the west door on the left has come out into the grand hall and been caught: now **two goblins sit tied up together** in a bundle with a strong rope (the rope from the second image), looking worried. They sit **on the flagstones of the grand hall itself**, in front of the west door's stone arch, at the foot of the tall candelabra, about a fifth of the way in from the left edge of the picture. They are clearly in the hall, closer to us than the doorway, **not** in the room beyond the door and not at the edge of the picture. Nobody peeks round the west door any more. **The west door and its arch stay exactly as they are**: the same width of stone arch, and the same wooden door with black iron hinges, hinged on the right-hand side of the arch and standing half open at the same angle, with the same candlelit room glimpsed beyond. Each goblin has its own two legs and feet. On the other side of the hall, **the goblin peeking round the east door stays exactly as he is**. Nothing else in the picture changes.
