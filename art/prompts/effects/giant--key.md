# giant--key

Shown when: "The way South is open." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> The way south behind the viewer is open, and cool daylight comes in **from behind us**, with a draught stirring the torch flames. In the middle of the bottom edge of the picture there is now **one patch of pale, silvery daylight** on the warm torchlit floor. Because the light comes from behind, it is **brightest at the very bottom edge**: the floor closest to us is lit first and most strongly, and the light spreads from there in a soft, narrow fan, reaching about a third of the way up the picture and fading as it goes. Nothing between the bottom edge and the patch is in shadow. Everything outside that patch stays as it was. Keep the cave and the giant exactly the same.
