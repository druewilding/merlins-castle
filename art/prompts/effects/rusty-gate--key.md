# rusty-gate--key

Shown when: "The gate swings open with a creak." (opens the way north)

The gate opens **into the yellow-flower meadow** (north), hinged at its outer
pillars, so here it swings away from us. `yellow-flowers--key` shows the same
open gate from the meadow side.

## Prompt

> The old rusty iron gate now **stands open**. Both halves have swung **away from us**, into the meadow beyond, each turning on its hinges at its own stone pillar (the left half hinged on the left, the right half on the right), so each now stands open at an angle, its spiked top seen foreshortened as it reaches away from us. Through the wide gap between them, the mist has parted a little, giving a glimpse of a sunny meadow covered in **tiny yellow flowers**. Nothing else in the picture changes.
