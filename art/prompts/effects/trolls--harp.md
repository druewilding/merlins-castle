# trolls--harp

Shown when: "The trolls dance and let you pass." (opens the way north)

## Prompt

> The trolls are **dancing**: up on their feet, arms linked, kicking up their heels with big happy grins, and the one with the tiny drum is beating it. Small golden musical notes float in the air. The north passage behind them is clear to walk through. Keep the cavern exactly the same.
