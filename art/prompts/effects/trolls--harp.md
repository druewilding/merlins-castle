# trolls--harp

Shown when: "The trolls dance and let you pass." (opens the way north)

## Prompt

> The big, shaggy, mossy cave trolls already in this picture (keep exactly their look: heavy, rock-like, old Scandinavian folk-tale trolls) have got up and are **stamping and swaying to music** in a clumsy, lumbering ring dance, arms linked and heads nodding, and the one with the tiny drum is beating it. They have moved aside, so the passage to the north behind them is clear. A faint golden shimmer hangs in the air, like the sound of a harp. Keep the cavern exactly the same.
