# moat--penny

Shown when: "The drawbridge lowers." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> The drawbridge is lowering: the two guards have **stepped aside**, one on each side of the path, and one is biting a shiny **penny** to check it's real while waving you through with his other hand. Chains glint as the end of the lowered drawbridge now rests between the two stone posts at the water's edge. Nothing else changes.
