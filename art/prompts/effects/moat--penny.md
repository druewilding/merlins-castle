# moat--penny

Shown when: "The drawbridge lowers." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> The drawbridge has been lowered: the two guards have **stepped aside**, one on each side of the path, and one gives a friendly wave to let you through. The **drawbridge** from the second image (the courtyard, seen from the castle side) now lies across the moat: the same wooden plank deck and iron chains, its near end resting on the bank between the two stone posts at the water's edge, and the deck running down across the water and out of the bottom edge of the picture, towards the castle behind us. Its two chains rise from its sides and leave the bottom edge too. Nothing else changes.
