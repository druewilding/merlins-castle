# giant--cake

Shown when: "The giant smiles. You can pass." (opens the way west)

## Prompt

> The giant has **stepped out of the west tunnel** and now sits on the cave floor beside it, just to the right of the tunnel mouth below the first torch, with his back against the cave wall. The **tunnel mouth on the left is now empty**: an open, dark way through with nobody in it. He is happily eating the **cake from the second image**, smiling, with crumbs in his beard. It must be exactly that cake, the same size as in his hands: pale cream frosting piped in swirls, whole dark red cherries with stems on top, chocolate shavings all over the sides, and golden sponge layers with cherry filling where a slice has been cut out. He holds the cake on one huge palm and a slice in the other hand. Keep the giant's own look and the cave exactly the same.
