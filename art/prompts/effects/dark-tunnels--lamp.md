# dark-tunnels--lamp

Shown when: "It is lighter. You can hear music." (opens the way south)

## Prompt

> It is **lighter**: warm golden lamplight now fills the crossroads, so the damp rock walls, the floor and all three tunnel mouths are clearly visible, with soft shadows. A faint golden shimmer drifts through the air, as if you can hear music. Keep every rock and tunnel in exactly the same place.
